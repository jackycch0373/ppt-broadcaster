Presenter Setup Guide

Step 1: Obtain the Plugin

Download Extension (.ppam)
  1. Security Unblock: Right-click the downloaded .ppam file > Properties. Check the Unblock checkbox at the bottom and click OK.
  2. Installation: Open PowerPoint, go to File > Options > Add-ins.
  3. Add-in Manager: Select PowerPoint Add-ins in the Manage dropdown at the bottom and click Go.
  4. Activate: Click Add New, select your file, and ensure PPT_Broadcaster is checked.
  5. Start: Click the "Live Web" tab now in your Ribbon and press Start Live.
